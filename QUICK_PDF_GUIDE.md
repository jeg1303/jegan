# 🚀 Quick PDF Generation (2 Minutes!)

## Simple Steps to Create Your Resume PDF:

### Step 1: Open Resume HTML
Double-click this file to open it in your browser:
```
c:\Users\jegan\OneDrive\Desktop\jegan\assets\resume.html
```

### Step 2: Print to PDF
- Press **Ctrl + P** on your keyboard
- In the printer selection, choose **"Save as PDF"** or **"Microsoft Print to PDF"**
- Click **"Save"**
- Navigate to: `c:\Users\jegan\OneDrive\Desktop\jegan\assets\`
- Name it: **resume.pdf**
- Click **"Save"**

### Step 3: Done! ✅
The download button on your website will now work!

---

## Alternative: Use Edge Browser

1. Open `assets/resume.html` in Microsoft Edge
2. Click the **three dots (...)** in top right
3. Click **"Print"**
4. Select **"Save as PDF"**
5. Save to `assets/resume.pdf`

---

## Test the Download

1. Run your website: `npm start`
2. Open: http://localhost:3000
3. Click the **"Download CV"** button in the hero section
4. Your resume PDF should download! 🎉

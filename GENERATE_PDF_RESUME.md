# 📄 How to Generate Your PDF Resume

You have a professional HTML resume ready. Here are 3 ways to create a PDF from it:

## Method 1: Using Your Browser (Easiest) ⭐

1. **Open the resume file:**
   - Navigate to: `c:\Users\jegan\OneDrive\Desktop\jegan\assets\resume.html`
   - Double-click to open in your browser

2. **Print to PDF:**
   - Press **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P on Mac)
   - In the printer selection, choose **"Save as PDF"** or **"Microsoft Print to PDF"**
   - Click **Save**
   - Save it as: `assets/resume.pdf`

3. **Done!** Your PDF is ready to download from your website.

---

## Method 2: Using Edge/Chrome Developer Tools

1. Open `assets/resume.html` in Edge or Chrome
2. Press **F12** to open Developer Tools
3. Press **Ctrl+Shift+P** to open Command Palette
4. Type "PDF" and select **"Print to PDF"**
5. Save as `assets/resume.pdf`

---

## Method 3: Online Converter (Quick)

1. Go to: https://www.web2pdfconvert.com/
2. Click "Choose File" and select `assets/resume.html`
3. Click "Convert to PDF"
4. Download and save as `assets/resume.pdf`
